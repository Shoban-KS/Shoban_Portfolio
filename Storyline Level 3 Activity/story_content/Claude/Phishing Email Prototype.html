Stop. Check. Report. Ask.

Use these four steps whenever a message asks you to act fast.

StopPause before you click or reply. Urgency is the trap.
CheckLook at the sender address, the link and the tone.
ReportSend it to ELB-IT-Admin. Do not forward it to colleagues.
AskUnsure? Ask IT using a channel you already trust.
